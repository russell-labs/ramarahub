About Ramara Hub

What this is

One simple place for Ramara residents to find what they need: who to call, how things work, and the real story behind the issues everyone is talking about. Township information is scattered and hard to find; the noise online makes it worse. This site exists to replace uncertainty with checkable facts.

Who runs it

Ramara Hub is a project of the Ramara Civic Forum, a resident group founded by Russell Cole after the December 2025 wastewater forums, to give residents a standing place to be heard. The Hub is independent and free. It is not affiliated with, or endorsed by, the Township of Ramara.

The rules this site follows

Facts only. Every answer and every brief links to an official source — township pages, bylaws, council minutes, or established local journalism. No guessing. If we don't have a sourced answer, we say so and give you the township's number. Both sides. On contested issues, the arguments for and against are presented as the people who made them actually said them, with attribution. No grades, no endorsements. Council pages show the public record only. Dated and verified. Every page shows when its information was last checked.

Spotted an error? Missing something?

Tell us and it gets fixed or added — that's how this site grows. Reach the Ramara Civic Forum through our Facebook group ↗, or contact the site team at russellcolevop@gmail.com.

What's coming

More issue briefs: housing developments, short-term rentals, geese, and property values. Councillor voting records from published minutes. And a plain-language ask-anything search that answers in full sentences. Watch this space.
