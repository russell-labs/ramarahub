Your council, your wards

2022–2026 term · Contacts as published on ramara.ca, verified June 11, 2026

Ramara has a 7-member council: the mayor and deputy mayor are elected by the whole township; each of the five wards elects one councillor. The current term ends November 14, 2026. The next election is Monday, October 26, 2026 (nominations open May 1 – August 21, 2026). Mayor Basil Clarke has announced he will not seek re-election. Which ward am I in?

The township publishes wards as legal boundary descriptions (Bylaw 2021.45) rather than community names. As a general guide: Ward 1 covers the north — the Washago area (Ramara side), Longford Mills, Cooper's Falls. Ward 2 is the large central-eastern rural band — Udney, Sebright, the Lake Dalrymple west shore. Ward 3 sits between the lakes — Atherley, Uptergrove, and nearby shoreline. Ward 4 is the south and southeast — rural south Ramara, the Gamebridge area, and Bayshore Village. Ward 5 is the Lake Simcoe shoreline strip — Lagoon City and Brechin.

This community guide is approximate — confirm your ward with the official map or the Clerk's office (705-484-5374 ext. 235) before relying on it, especially near boundaries.

Meetings: when, where, how to watch

Council / Committee of the Whole meets twice monthly on Mondays at 9:30 a.m. (once only in July, August, and December) at the Administration Building, 2297 Highway 12, Brechin. Agendas are posted the Friday before on CivicWeb ↗; meetings stream live on YouTube ↗. [Meetings page] [2026 schedule PDF]

How to be heard

Deputation (present for up to 10 minutes): submit the request form ↗ with your materials by noon the Monday before the meeting. Open Forum (1 minute, current agenda items): register ↗ by 9 a.m. on meeting day. Petitions must use the township template ↗. Clerk's office: 705-484-5374 ext. 235, clerks@ramara.ca. You can also apply to join a township committee ↗ — there are 18+, from Climate Action to Trails to the Youth Action Council.
