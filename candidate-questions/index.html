2026 municipal election

What would you like to ask the candidates?

Send in a question for Ramara's mayoral and deputy mayoral candidates. Contact details are optional.

Questions may be combined or shortened for clarity. We cannot promise that every question will be asked.

You can submit without contact details. If provided, they are stored privately with your question by Ramara Civic Forum organizers and used only to clarify it. They are not published, shared with candidates, or added to a mailing list. They will be deleted after question follow-up ends, no later than October 31, 2026. Email russellcolevop@gmail.com to have them deleted sooner. Website and database providers may receive standard technical information, such as your IP address, to deliver the form. Read the privacy details.

Ramara Hub is independent and does not support or oppose any candidate. Submitted questions are reviewed for relevance, clarity, and respectful language. Candidate offices are verified against the Township of Ramara's certified candidate list ↗.
Ramara Hub is an independent, free community resource. It is not the Township of Ramara's official website.
