Asked the Township

A public log of what the Ramara Civic Forum has asked — and how long answers take.

Residents deserve to see what gets asked of their township and how long it takes to get an answer. This page is that record: every formal question the Ramara Civic Forum puts to the township, in writing, with its status and a dated timeline of any response. When the township answers, the answer goes here too.

This is accountability, not attack. Most questions are simple requests for information that should be public anyway. We post them so the asking is visible — and so a slow answer, or no answer, is visible too. Tap any question to see the full request and its updates.
