Your questions don't just get answered — they get heard

Every question asked here is counted (anonymously — we record the question, never who asked it). The issues raised most often, with the biggest impact on residents, are researched into full briefs and brought to the township through the Ramara Civic Forum — the resident group built to do exactly that. Asking a question here is a small act of making Ramara work better.
