What's new

What's new on the Hub

Ramara Hub is built and improved in the open. Here's what's been added and fixed, newest first — so you can see the site is actively maintained, not set-and-forgotten.

June 12, 2026

Township news feed & weekly updates

  • Added a live Latest from the Township feed — the newest council updates and notices from ramara.ca, dated and newest-first, each linked to the official post.
  • The home page now shows the three latest township items with a link to the full feed.
  • You can sign up for a free weekly email and tell us the topics you care about (wastewater, roads, Lagoon City…) so we can flag what's relevant to you.
June 12, 2026

The Record & Great things about Ramara

  • Launched The Record — a searchable library of 900+ public township documents (bylaws, council highlights, reports, plans), each linked to its official source.
  • Added Great things about Ramara, a community media wall celebrating the good happening around the township.
June 12, 2026

Ramara's Money & Asked the Township

  • Added Ramara's Money — a plain-language look at the township budget, with the $42.9M operating-plus-capital-plus-reserves picture reconciled and the records we're still asking the township to make public.
  • Added Asked the Township — a public log of the questions we've put to the township and the replies as they come back.
June 12, 2026

Issue Briefs: the big topics, sourced

  • Published full, sourced issue briefs on wastewater, roads, and the sports dome.
  • Added a court-sourced, neutral brief on the Lagoon City shore walls and contempt ruling.
  • Added the publishing standard — exactly how the Hub decides what to publish — so the neutrality rules are public.
June 11, 2026

Ramara Hub launched

  • Ask-in-your-own-words search, backed by a curated knowledge base and full-text search across the township record — and it never guesses.
  • Council & wards information, an everyday-answers topic browser, and a mobile menu.
  • An anonymous feedback and follow-up system: thumbs on every answer, and the option to leave your email for a better answer when one's missing.
Ongoing

Continual answer fixes & additions

  • Answers are added and corrected as residents ask and flag things — recent additions include the Lagoon City noise bylaw, pet limits, illegal dumping, short-term-rental setbacks, and septic-to-sewer connection questions, each tied to its source.
  • Ongoing accuracy and mobile-experience tuning across the whole site.

Spotted something wrong or missing? Tell us — that's how the Hub grows.
