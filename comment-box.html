The Comment Box

A simple, neutral place to say what's on your mind about your corner of Ramara. Pick your ward, drop in a comment, and it reaches the right people. Like dropping a note in a box at the front of the hall.

The Comment Box is unavailable while Ramara Hub is read-only. No comment or contact information is collected on this site. You can contact your councillor through the Council & Wards page.
The public page remains here so old links explain the service status. No new comments are accepted or shared from this site.
