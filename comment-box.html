The Comment Box

A simple, neutral place to say what's on your mind about your corner of Ramara. Pick your ward, drop in a comment, and it reaches the right people. Like dropping a note in a box at the front of the hall.

Not sure which ward you're in? Check the Township's ward map.

Leaving your email lets us or your councillor follow up. Skip it if you'd rather not.

Comments are sorted by ward and shared with the councillor who represents it. This is a neutral community resource, not the Township's official site, and it doesn't support or oppose anyone. We read everything; we reply when you've left a way to reach you.

A councillor reading this: we built the Comment Box to make hearing from your ward easier, not harder. If you'd like the comments from your ward, or want to help shape how this works, reach out at russellcolevop@gmail.com. The goal is to make your job a little easier.
